# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone — regardless of background or identity.

## Standards

- Be kind, respectful, constructive
- No harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Stay on-topic, help newcomers

## Enforcement

Report to `ikalamin0@gmail.com`. All reports confidential, reviewed promptly. Maintainers may remove comments, issues, or bans for violations.
